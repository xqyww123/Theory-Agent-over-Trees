## Documentation quality

Write for the reader. Before each sentence, ask whether they need it.

- **Not from your own head.** Do not write down what you happened to think of,
  in the order you thought of it. Decide what the reader needs, then write that.
- **No filler.** Padding, throat-clearing, restatement, and sentences that only
  signal effort are defects. Shorter is better when it says the same thing.
- **Nothing the reader cannot use.** Detail that answers no question and changes
  no decision does not belong, however true it is.
- **No change-rationale.** Why something changed, what it used to be, and what
  alternative was passed over go in the commit message. A document says what is.
- **Read it back as the reader.** A sentence leaning on a term, abbreviation, or
  context they may not have must be explained or removed.

This applies to every document here, including this one.
